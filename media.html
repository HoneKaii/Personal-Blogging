How to add photos/videos (Netlify-friendly)

  1. Add files to media/photos/ in this repo (for example media/photos/rehearsal.jpg).
  2. Open media-config.js and add an item with type: "image" or type: "video".
  3. Commit and push. Netlify redeploys automatically.

Google Photos option

Keep using a Google Photos shared album by adding a type: "link" entry in media-config.js.
